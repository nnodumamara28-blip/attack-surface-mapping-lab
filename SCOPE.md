## Permitted techniques
- Host discovery
- TCP port scanning (top 100 ports, then service identification on open ports)
- Manual service exposure review
- Local listening-service review on machines I own
- HTTP header inspection of authorized web services
- Passive packet capture inside the lab network

## Prohibited activities
- Exploitation of any vulnerability
- Password attacks or brute forcing
- Denial-of-service testing
- Bypassing authentication or accessing confidential data
- Vulnerability exploitation scripts or intrusive scan options
- Scanning public IP addresses, school networks, home or office networks, or third-party systems
